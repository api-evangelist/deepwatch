# Deepwatch

Deepwatch provides AI-native managed detection and response (MDR) governed by experienced security experts. The company helps organizations detect, investigate, contain, and respond to threats across existing security environments while maintaining transparency, accountability, and human oversight.

Official website: https://www.deepwatch.com/
Contact: info@deepwatch.com, +1-855-303-3033

## Core positioning

- AI-native MDR governed by expert human analysts.
- Proactive investigation, containment, and response across existing security environments.
- Transparent security operations with no black boxes and expert accountability.
- MDR outcomes supported by NEXA AI, threat intelligence, exposure management, analyst expertise, and customer-specific context.
- Deepwatch works with existing SIEM, endpoint, cloud, identity, SaaS, and network environments without requiring rip-and-replace programs.
